# Oklahoma Rural Solar scoring model

Version `ok-rural-solar-v1.0` totals 100 points:

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Land affordability | 12 |
| Usable acreage | 8 |
| Cleared/pasture condition | 8 |
| Slope/topography | 8 |
| Flood exposure | 8 |
| Wetlands/environmental constraints | 8 |
| Avoidance of prime farmland | 5 |
| Legal road access | 6 |
| Three-phase/distribution proximity | 12 |
| Interconnection confidence | 10 |
| Zoning/permitting fit | 5 |
| Nearby commercial load/off-taker fit | 4 |
| Seller flexibility/site-control fit | 3 |
| Strategic county fit | 3 |

Unknown fields receive limited or zero points. Missing utility evidence materially lowers confidence. Flood/wetland exposure and absent legal access create visible flags. Scores support screening; they are not engineering, environmental, legal, investment, or utility determinations.

Categories are Priority Candidate (85+), Strong Candidate (70–84), Research Further (55–69), High Risk (40–54), and Reject or Archive (below 40). Human overrides never replace the computed score and require a written reason.
